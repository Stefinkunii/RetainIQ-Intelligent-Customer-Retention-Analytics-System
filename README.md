# RetainIQ – Intelligent Customer Retention & Analytics System

**RetainIQ** is an intelligent, data-driven customer retention and analytics system designed to help businesses understand customer purchasing behavior, identify customers at risk of churn, and improve retention strategies through machine learning and predictive analytics.

The system analyzes historical customer transactions, identifies purchasing patterns, segments customers based on their behavior, and predicts potential customer churn. By combining analytical insights with personalized retention recommendations, RetainIQ aims to help businesses make informed decisions, strengthen customer relationships, and reduce customer loss.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and transaction history.
- Identify and segment customers using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis.
- Predict customer churn using machine learning techniques.
- Calculate customer retention risk scores.
- Provide explainable insights into customer churn predictions.
- Recommend personalized retention strategies and interventions.
- Visualize customer trends and business performance through interactive dashboards.

## 🚀 Key Features & Modules

RetainIQ consists of 12 core modules:

1. **Customer & Transaction Data Management** – Manage customer records and transaction datasets.
2. **Data Preprocessing & Validation** – Clean, validate, and prepare data for analysis.
3. **Customer Purchase Analytics** – Analyze purchasing trends and customer behavior.
4. **RFM Analysis** – Evaluate customers based on recency, frequency, and monetary value.
5. **Customer Segmentation** – Group customers according to purchasing characteristics.
6. **Customer Churn Prediction** – Identify customers likely to stop purchasing.
7. **Retention Risk Scoring** – Assign risk scores to customers based on predictive insights.
8. **Explainable Customer Risk Analysis** – Explain factors contributing to customer churn.
9. **Customer 360° Profile** – Provide a comprehensive view of individual customer behavior.
10. **What-If Risk Simulator** – Simulate how changes in customer behavior may influence churn risk.
11. **Retention Recommendation & Intervention** – Suggest targeted strategies to retain customers.
12. **Retention Analytics Dashboard & Reporting** – Present insights through visual reports and dashboards.
